# Scarlet Veil — Crimson Blade & combat VFX overhaul

Branch `arena/175fcd9f-waifu`. Visual direction: crimson energy identity
(deep crimson `#790018`, scarlet `#FF1744`, bright red `#FF365D`, highlight
`#FF8295`, white-hot core `#FFF0F2`) replacing the old violet/white look.

## Phase 1 — audit findings (current architecture, verified in workspace @ ad6291c)

**Weapon**
- `player.gd::_attach_sword()`: sword is a `BoneAttachment3D` on `J_Bip_R_Hand`
  + a `grip` Node3D at the hand origin (offset `(0.038, 0, -0.005)`, base
  rotation `(0°, 8°, 35°)`); the blade is built **procedurally from
  primitives** (`_make_blade`, `_sword_cylinder/sphere/piece`) and extends
  along **-Y** (outline y +0.05 … -0.88, total ≈ 0.93 m). A hex-cylinder
  **aura shell** (violet, additive) hugs the blade and pulses in `_animate`.
  Fingers are curled via `_pose_gripping_fingers()`.
  → Gap vs brief: placeholder primitives, violet identity. Plan: real
  `assets/crimson_blade.glb` (generated by `tools/make_crimson_blade.py`),
  same grip node/orientation, scarlet emission channel in the blade material.

**Combat events (all in `world.gd`, driven by `player.gd` signals)**
- `player.attack_swing(stage)` → `world._swing_vfx(stage)`: spawns
  `combat_vfx` kinds `slash` (always), `echo` (stage≥2), `finisher`
  (stage≥3), `cleave` (stage 4), rotated to `facing`.
- `player.hit_enemy` → `_hit_enemy`: `impact` sparks at the enemy + camera kick.
- `player.dodge()` → `_spawn_vfx("dodge", …)`.
- `_spawn_vfx` caps live effects in group `combat_effects` (budget 12 FULL /
  6 REDUCED) and frees oldest beyond budget.
- `combat_vfx.gd`: one-shot `Node3D` with an `ImmediateMesh`, vertex-colored,
  additive unshaded material, `queue_free()` after `lifetime` (0.30–0.42 s).
  Kinds: slash/echo/finisher/cleave/impact/dodge — all violet palette.
- `sword_trail.gd`: `MeshInstance3D` ribbon sampling the actual grip global
  transform (base y −0.02, tip y −0.86) while the attack window is active;
  12 samples × 0.14 s. Color today: violet-white.
- **Quality**: `settings.gd` has `effects` 0/1 (REDUCED/FULL) with a HUD
  toggle; `combat_vfx.setup(..., quality)` drops petals/feather layers at 0
  and `_spawn_vfx` lowers the budget. Physics quality (0/1/2) is separate.
  → New effects will reuse this exact routing.

**Animation/combat mechanics to preserve**: `player.attack()` combo 1–4
(`ATTACK_DURATION` 0.58 s, hit at ≤0.60 of the timer), `dodge()` dash,
`energy` 0–100 (+20 per hit, currently unconsumed — free hook for
skill/ultimate), authored combo clips, wiggle physics, HUD layout.

**Rendering/perf constraints**: GL Compatibility renderer (also used by CI
software rendering), Mali-G72 target; existing approach is bounded
ImmediateMesh + no dynamic lights + `cast_shadow OFF` — kept. Particles will
be tiny `CPUParticles3D` bursts (8–16) parented to the one-shot effect nodes
so they are freed with them and never hit the effect budget/group counts.
